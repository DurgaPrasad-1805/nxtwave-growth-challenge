## Slide 1: Target Persona & Core Value Proposition

### The Final-Year Reality
- **Target Audience:** Final-year engineering students (CSE, ECE, Mech, Civil) in Tier-2/3/4 Indian engineering colleges.
- **The Core Anxiety:** Placements are ongoing/approaching. Mass recruiters have slowed hiring. Resumes are filled with generic college projects (Library System, Calculator) that fail modern ATS filters.
- **The Resistance:** Skeptical of long webinars that teach high-level AI theory without practical takeaways.

### The Irresistible Hook
> **“Don’t learn AI theory. Build and deploy a real AI app to your GitHub in 60 minutes and leave with 3 ATS-ready resume bullets for your next placement interview.”**

### Key Triggers for Conversion:
1. **Zero-friction 1-click WhatsApp entry** (no 10-question Google Forms).
2. **Proof-of-Work guarantee:** Live hosted URL + GitHub repo.
3. **Immediate value:** Instant branch-customized project preview before the session.

## Slide 2: The 7-Day Growth Engine (Prioritized Channels)

*We reject spraying across 20 weak channels. We execute 3 high-conviction loops:*

### Loop 1: The Class Representative (CR) Placement Guild (Goal: 280 Registrations)
- **Mechanic:** Mobilize 35 CRs and Student Placement Coordinators across 20 targeted engineering colleges.
- **Asset:** Provide an official "Placement Cell Skill Circular" copy formatted directly for batch WhatsApp groups (open rate >90%).
- **Conversion:** 35 groups × 80 students avg × 10% conversion = **280 registrations**.

### Loop 2: "Unlock-the-Vault" Viral Referral Loop (Goal: 160 Registrations)
- **Mechanic:** Post-registration, students get an instant referral code.
- **Incentive:** *"Invite 2 batchmates to join the sprint and instantly unlock the 2026 AI Capstone & Interview Vault (50+ architecture diagrams & code repos)."*
- **Viral Math:** Viral coefficient $K = 0.32$ across 500 participants = **160 referred registrations**.

### Loop 3: The ₹2,000 Micro-Bounty Leverage (Goal: 80 Registrations)
- Rather than burning ₹2,000 on low-conversion Meta ads (est. 15-20 leads), the ₹2,000 is leveraged for student distribution incentives:
  - **₹1,200:** CR Leaderboard Vouchers (Top 3 CRs bringing most batch registrations get ₹600, ₹400, ₹200 Amazon cards).
  - **₹800:** Two live ₹400 lucky-draw vouchers during the workshop to drive a >70% attendance show-up rate.

## Slide 5: Strategic Judgment & Meta-Learning

### 1. What Changed Between First Idea and Final Solution?
- **Initial Idea:** Run Meta Advantage+ lead-generation ads targeted at Indian tech colleges.
- **The Pivot:** Fast mathematical audit showed Indian student CPL on Meta is ₹25–₹60. A ₹2,000 budget would yield at most 40–50 registrations (a 90% failure). We completely pivoted to a high-leverage **CR Placement Network + Viral Referral Loop** where ₹2,000 powers gamified micro-bounties.

### 2. What Did AI Suggest That We Deliberately Rejected & Why?
- **AI Suggestion:** Offer a "Certificate of Participation" to all attendees.
- **Rejection Rationale:** Tier-2/3 final-year students have dozens of worthless participation certificates that recruiters ignore. Replacing the certificate with **a live deployed GitHub link and ATS-tested resume bullet points** increased perceived utility tenfold.
- **AI Suggestion:** Automated LinkedIn scraping and InMail outreach.
- **Rejection Rationale:** Engineering students check LinkedIn sporadically; InMail response rate is <12%. WhatsApp is their daily communication center.

### 3. What Would We Improve with 24 More Hours?
- Deploy a native 2-way WhatsApp Bot via Meta Cloud API / Gupshup for frictionless zero-browser registration.
- Integrate an inter-college live leaderboard (e.g., CBIT vs. SRM vs. VNR VJIET) to drive organic college rivalries.
