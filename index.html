Messaging & Value Prop EXPERIMENT 01

Placement Armor vs. Generic Tech Stack Hook

Hypothesis: Positioning around "Placement Armor & 3 ATS Bullets" will generate higher click-to-registration conversion than generic "Learn Generative AI & LLMs" messaging.
What We Test: A/B test 2 distinct WhatsApp broadcast copies across 20 college groups using dedicated UTM parameters (Variant A: Outcome/Placement Hook vs. Variant B: Skill/Tech Hook).
Primary Metric: Click-to-Registration Conversion Rate (Form Submissions / Unique Link Clicks).
Secondary Metric: Unique Click-Through Rate (CTR) in college WhatsApp groups.
Success Threshold: Variant A achieves ≥25% higher relative conversion rate than Variant B.
Decision: If Variant A wins, lock placement-first messaging across all Day 5–6 retargeting pushes; if Variant B wins, pivot messaging to emphasize technical LLM depth and modern AI skills.
Distribution & Channel Authority EXPERIMENT 02

CR Broadcasts vs. Open Community Channels

Hypothesis: Promotional messages distributed directly by Class Representatives (CRs) in official batch WhatsApp groups deliver ≥3× more verified signups per reach than open student Discord/Telegram channels.
What We Test: 15 peer CR broadcasts vs. 5 regional Discord/Telegram placement communities of similar reach (~1,500 reach each), tracked via UTM tags.
Primary Metric: Total Verified Registrations per 1,000 Reach.
Secondary Metric: Registration Completion Speed (time from broadcast to first 50 verified signups).
Success Threshold: CR WhatsApp generates ≥3× more verified registrations per reach than open Discord/Telegram groups.
Decision: If CR WhatsApp wins, allocate 100% of the ₹1,200 bounty pool to the CR performance leaderboard; if Discord/Telegram performs strongly, divert ₹400 to incentivize Discord moderators.
Referral Mechanics & K-Factor EXPERIMENT 03
