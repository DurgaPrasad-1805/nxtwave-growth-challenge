Message Hook TEST 01

Placement Armor vs. Tech Stack

Primary: Form Conv % | Secondary: Link CTR

Decision: If Variant A wins, lock placement-first messaging for all retargeting pushes; if Variant B wins, emphasize technical LLM depth.
Channel Authority TEST 02

CR Broadcasts vs. Open Discord/Telegram

Hypothesis: Official CR batch WhatsApp circulars deliver ≥3× more verified signups per reach than open student Discord communities.
What We Test: 15 CR batch broadcasts vs. 5 regional Discord/Telegram placement communities (~1,500 reach each).
Primary: Verified Regs / 1k Reach | Secondary: Time-to-50-Signups
Threshold: ≥3× higher registration rate on CR WhatsApp.
Decision: If CR WhatsApp wins, allocate 100% of ₹1,200 bounty to CR leaderboard; if Discord performs, reallocate ₹400 to incentivize Discord moderators.
Referral Mechanics TEST 03

Digital Milestone Vault vs. Cash Lottery

Hypothesis: A guaranteed Milestone Digital Vault unlock (2 referrals) drives higher K-factor (≥0.30) than a probabilistic lottery raffle.
What We Test: 50/50 split on post-registration screen: Digital Capstone Vault vs. ₹500 Amazon Voucher Lucky Draw entry.
Primary: Viral K-Factor | Secondary: WhatsApp Share Button CTR
Threshold: Milestone Vault achieves K ≥ 0.30 (vs. lottery K < 0.15).
Decision: If Milestone Vault wins, lock all viral mechanics to digital asset gating; if lottery wins, shift referral rewards to cash vouchers.
